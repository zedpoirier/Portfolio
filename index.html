PROJECTS

Cloudhead Games

Visual Effects Shaders Tooling

During my time at Cloudhead Games I worked on a VR title built for Meta Quest, contributing real-time shaders, gameplay-driven VFX, and pipeline tooling across a fast-moving production.


A highlight was implementing a multi-stage enemy respawn sequence — a mechanic with layered narrative and mechanical timing that required coordinating directly with engineering to map game state transitions, then authoring a dissolve shader system that handled material swaps, drove shader properties, and fired particle sequences in precise sync with each phase.


On the tools side, I built a normal map packing utility that merged main and detail maps into a single RGBA texture, reducing a material from 12+ texture samples and meaningfully improving GPU performance. Due to NDA I can't share visuals publicly, but I'm happy to walk through the work in detail during a call.
